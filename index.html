Zotter is an answer to a world in which every digital writing surface is a bright, light-emitting screen designed to distract you.

It is a single-purpose, anti-corporate, anti-productive, anti-AI, free/libre writing and note-taking device, handbuilt by one person in a small workshop in Cambridge, UK.

Zotter is designed for people who work with words: writers, poets, playwrights, academics, students, teachers, programmers.

Zotter is still under development. You can follow its progress on our /blog.
